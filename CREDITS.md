# Credits

DrumCoach is written by Michael Olsen.

## Teaching approach

Rudiment names and stickings follow the Percussive Arts Society list. The
order of the lessons follows the approach of George Lawrence Stone's *Stick
Control for the Snare Drummer* and Carmine Appice's *Realistic Rock*. The
lessons in the app are written in that spirit and are not copied from the
books, which remain the references to practise from.
